Ground-level XL-MIMO Dataset

The CSI-EnvFormer dataset pairs ray-traced channel state information (CSI) with base-station RGB and LiDAR observations and UE position and motion records. It covers line-of-sight (LoS) mobility at 2, 5, 10, and 20 m/s in simulated urban layouts.

Channel and sensing records

Channels are simulated at 7 GHz with Sionna RT. The wireless slot is 1 ms, and sensing updates arrive every 10 ms.

Complex CSI

10 historical CSI slots and 12 targets at 1, 5, 10, 20, …, 100 ms.

Multi-view RGB

Three 384 × 384 views from left, center, and right base-station cameras, rendered with Mitsuba.

LiDAR

Co-located 64 × 768 ray-cast scans with an 80 m maximum range.

Position and motion

Timestamped UE coordinates and velocity aligned with the sensing frames.

Ray-traced path labels are included in the training, validation, and seen-layout test splits.
